## My Philosophy

In early 2022, I started **OpensRun**:  
- **Open**: stay curious and open‑minded  
- **Run**: keep moving forward in uncertainty  

I previously tried to build an education brand, and from that journey I realized:  
- Education should not revolve only around scores.  
- Technology should not become a tool for creating anxiety.  

With the rapid development of AI, the value of “score‑only mindset” is further weakened.  
So I chose to **return to product and engineering themselves**, and focus my energy on:

> Building truly valuable tools that can accompany users for the long term,  
> Letting AI and software **reduce burden and increase efficiency, instead of amplifying anxiety**.
